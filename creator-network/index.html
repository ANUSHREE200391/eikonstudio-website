How it works

Simple, and handled by us.

The creator network is a managed service. Our team handles every match and collaboration personally. You won't need to sign up to a platform.

  1. Enquiry

    Brands share their goals. Creators share their profile and category.

  2. Matching

    We shortlist creators whose content and audience fit the brand.

  3. Coordination

    We align on the brief, timelines and deliverables, and keep everyone in sync.

  4. Delivery

    Content goes live and the campaign runs, with our team coordinating throughout.
